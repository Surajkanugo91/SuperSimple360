Before we build

Questions worth
asking first.

Clear answers about fit, scope, timing and ongoing support help avoid expensive technology experiments.

01What happens during the free workflow audit?+

We discuss one repetitive or slow process, understand how it works today and identify whether a practical AI or automation solution is worth exploring. You receive a recommended next step with no obligation to proceed.

02Does my business need to be technically advanced?+

No. We start with the work your team already performs, explain the approach in business language and shape the system around a manageable level of complexity.

03Can you work with our current tools?+

Usually, yes. We assess your tools, data access and constraints first. Where practical, we connect what you already use instead of adding another disconnected platform.

04How long does a solution take to build?+

It depends on workflow complexity, integrations, data quality and testing needs. After the audit, we propose a defined first version and realistic delivery plan.

05How do you approach business data and security?+

We map what data is required, who should access it and where human approval is needed. The final design and tool choice are agreed around your security requirements.

06What happens after delivery?+

We provide a practical handover and can support adoption, monitoring and improvements. The ongoing support model is agreed as part of the engagement.
